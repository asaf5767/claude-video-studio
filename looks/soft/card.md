Use this look when the creator wants their reel to feel calm, trustworthy and a little intimate -- a reflection, a personal story, advice given gently, or anything where "slow down and listen" is the point. It reads as warm and premium, not flashy.

Practical notes for editing:
- Keep cuts rare. If in doubt, hold a beat longer rather than cutting again.
- The Bellefair title card is the one moment of typographic presence in the whole reel -- use it once per scene change at most, and let it breathe (fade in over ~0.5s, sit still, fade out).
- Grade warm: slightly lifted shadows, a touch of amber/cream in the highlights. Avoid cold or clinical color.
- Captions should feel like subtitles, not graphics: small, legible, out of the way, never the visual focus.
- If the creator's footage has jump cuts or rough audio edits, prefer a gentle cross-dissolve to smooth them rather than hiding them with a flashy transition -- flash would break the calm tone.
- SFX exists to mark a scene change quietly, not to add energy. When unsure whether a sound belongs, leave it out.
