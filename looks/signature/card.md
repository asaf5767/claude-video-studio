---
id: signature
name_he: חתימה
name_en: Signature
captions: One clean line at a time, static (no pop, no karaoke), in a bold modern sans. Key words only are emphasized with a single accent-color underline or a highlighter-marker stroke -- the rest of the line stays plain. Max 28 characters per line. RTL by default; wrap any Latin word or run of digits in one dir=ltr isolate span so mixed Hebrew/English/numbers never reorder or merge letters.
titles: A clean, confident sans-serif title card, left- or center-aligned, with exactly one accent color used for an underline or a marker stroke beneath the key word. No gradients, no drop shadows beyond a subtle one for legibility.
pace: Steady and deliberate. A cut or beat change roughly every 2-3 seconds -- confident, not rushed and not languid.
transitions: A simple cut or a quick cross-fade (0.2-0.3s). No wipes, no paper tears, no glitch -- the transitions should be almost invisible so the one accent color and the underline moments stand out instead.
graphics: A single recurring accent color used consistently throughout (chosen per video, not fixed), simple underline/marker strokes under key words or phrases, occasional plain icon call-outs. No stickers, no textures, no competing colors.
music: Light, modern and minimal -- a simple beat or a clean synth bed that stays out of the way.
sfx: A single soft marker-squeak or tick exactly when an underline or emphasis mark draws in. Otherwise quiet.
hook: The subject speaks directly and plainly to camera; the title card appears with the accent-color underline landing under the one word that matters.
avoid: Using more than one accent color in the same video, stickers or paper textures, more than one graphic effect on the same beat, loud or busy music, long captions.
---

Use this look when the creator wants a consistent, professional "brand" feel across their reels -- expertise content, personal brand building, anything where looking put-together and recognizable matters more than looking flashy.

Practical notes for editing:
- Pick one accent color for the whole video up front (ask the creator, or infer from their footage/wardrobe/brand) and use it everywhere an accent is needed: underlines, the title's key word, maybe one icon stroke. Never introduce a second accent color mid-video.
- The underline/marker-stroke emphasis is the one recurring signature move -- use it on the single most important word per beat, not on every word.
- Keep cuts clean and simple; the discipline of "no wipes, no stickers, no textures" is what makes this look read as a signature style rather than "no style at all."
- If the creator's talking points have a clear structure (three tips, two options, a before/after), a simple numbered or labeled title card in the same clean sans reinforces the signature feel.
- When unsure whether to add a graphic, default to not adding one -- this look earns its polish through restraint and consistency, not density of effects.
