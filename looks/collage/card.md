Use this look when the creator wants their reel to feel fun, handmade and shareable -- tips, listicles, reactions, anything with a playful or DIY energy. It should feel like a scrapbook come to life, not a polished ad.

Practical notes for editing:
- Treat every beat as an opportunity for one small tactile arrival: a sticker, a torn-paper caption chip, a doodled arrow pointing at something. Keep each individual element simple -- the energy comes from frequency, not from any one element being elaborate.
- Keep elements off the subject's face; cluster them in the upper third or the margins around the frame.
- Match the sound to the material: paper things rustle, tape things peel, pointer things squeak. Never use a digital "whoosh" here -- it breaks the handmade feel.
- Vary tilt and placement scene to scene so nothing feels like a repeating template; a little visual chaos is the point, as long as text stays readable.
- If the footage is calm or static, this look still works by adding motion through graphics rather than through camera moves -- don't force punch-ins or shake to compensate.
